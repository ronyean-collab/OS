## Feature overview (landing sections)

### Persistent workspaces
Organize threads and messages in projects that survive restarts.

### In-app chat with Ollama
Run models locally when you want chat inside the app — no cloud required.

### Manual Mode
Copy a context pack to ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, or any tool — paste the reply back. Continuity stays local.

### Backups & restore
Export encrypted backups. Preview imports. Roll back if something looks wrong.

### Recovery & integrity
Built for real-world failure: crashes, cancellations, upgrades.

### Local diagnostics
Health signals for runtime, recovery, providers, startup, and migrations — no cloud telemetry.

## FAQ

**Is ContinuityOS a ChatGPT replacement?**  
No. It is a continuity layer. Use any AI you like; ContinuityOS keeps the thread, backups, and recovery on your machine.

**Does my data go to the cloud?**  
Not by default. In-app chat uses Ollama locally. Manual Mode is copy/paste — you control what leaves the device.

**Do you train on my messages?**  
No cloud training pipeline in ContinuityOS. Your database is local.

**Can I sync between laptop and desktop?**  
Not in v1. Use encrypted exports you control.

**Do you support agents or plugins?**  
Not in v1. Scope is calm continuity, not autonomous agents.

**What platforms?**  
Windows, macOS, and Linux (Electron).

**Is it free?**  
Beta builds are free; future tiers are ideas only — see [PRICING-IDEAS.md](./PRICING-IDEAS.md).
