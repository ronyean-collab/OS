# ContinuityOS Business Vision

**Status:** Strategic business direction — not a financial plan or pricing sheet

## Company vision

**The Continuity Layer for AI**

ContinuityOS is building the durable personal layer beneath commoditized intelligence — where users own their AI life and assistants persist across models, providers, and years.

**Public positioning:** *It's like ChatGPT, but it remembers.*

**Product category:** Personal AI Operating System

---

## Target users

### Primary persona — The long-horizon builder

Someone who uses AI for **projects that span weeks or months**: software, writing, research, planning, learning, creative work. They have been burned by lost tabs, reset threads, or vendor lock-in.

**Needs:** continuity, ownership, one assistant, local trust

### Secondary persona — The local-first privacy user

Someone who prefers **Ollama or offline work** but wants better persistence than a terminal session.

**Needs:** default local path, optional cloud, no surveillance

### Tertiary persona — The multi-provider power user

Someone who uses **ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and local models** but wants **one continuous AI life**.

**Needs:** provider independence, Manual Mode or unified continuity, export

---

## Year 1 users

Year 1 is **beta and daily-driver validation**, not mass consumer scale.

**Year 1 focus cohorts:**

1. **Local AI early adopters** — Ollama users who want a real workspace
2. **Continuity-frustrated professionals** — lost work to session-based tools
3. **Privacy-conscious knowledge workers** — want assistant utility without cloud custody
4. **Indie builders and writers** — long projects, single-user, export-sensitive

**Year 1 success looks like:**

- Users complete onboarding without hand-holding
- Backup/restore trusted in real use
- "The conversation never dies" is **felt**, not just marketed
- Retention driven by **returning to ongoing projects**, not novelty

**Year 1 is not:** enterprise sales, team collab, agent marketplace, or cloud-sync-by-default.

---

## Long-term users

Long-term, ContinuityOS serves anyone who treats AI as **part of personal or professional infrastructure** — not a toy.

**Expanding segments (future):**

- Consultants and solo professionals with client projects
- Researchers with multi-month literature and analysis threads
- Creators with serialized creative continuity
- Eventually: small teams needing **shared export bundles**, not live collab (only if constitution-aligned)

**Long-term relationship:** Users stay because **their AI life compounds** — years of context, decisions, and assistant trust — and they **own it**.

|-------------|----------------|------------|
| **ChatGPT** | Best-in-class hosted chat | You don't own the history; sessions reset |
| **Claude** | Reasoning quality | Same — excellent model, fragile personal continuity |

## Monetization philosophy

**Monetize power and peace of mind — never monetize ownership of truth.**

**We charge for:**

- Advanced continuity tooling (when built)
- Priority support and production polish
- Optional convenience (backup vault with user keys, update channel)
- Commercial clarity for business use

**We do not charge for:**

- Recovery after failure
- Export of user's AI life
- Core Manual Mode loop
- Migration safety
- Basic local continuity

See [PRICING-IDEAS.md](./PRICING-IDEAS.md) for tier sketches. **No billing ships without aligning to this philosophy.**

**Anti-pattern:** Lock-in subscriptions where export is crippled or history is held hostage.

---

## Pricing philosophy

1. **Free tier must be genuinely useful** — credibility of "user ownership" requires it
2. **Ollama-first path stays free** — local default is not a trial gimmick
3. **Paid APIs remain optional** — user brings keys or uses Manual Mode
4. **Pro tiers sell acceleration and support**, not fundamental continuity
5. **Founder/beta goodwill** — early adopters who bet on continuity should be honored

Price sensitivity is lower when **switching cost is exportable**. Our business must embrace that — exportability builds trust, trust builds retention.

---

## Economic moat

**Why continuity matters commercially:**

- Intelligence **commoditizes** — models get cheaper and interchangeable
- **Continuity compounds** — switching cost rises with owned AI life *if* user trusts ownership
- **Trust compounds** — honest assistant over years beats clever one-shot replies
- **Provider neutrality** — we capture value across model vendor churn

**Moat layers:**

1. **Data moat (user-owned):** The user's AI life lives locally — we win by being the best manager of it, not by trapping it
2. **Identity moat:** One assistant across providers — hard to replicate after years of trusted dialogue
3. **Recovery moat:** Engineering depth in persistence, migration, rebuild — boring until competitors lose user data
4. **Philosophy moat:** Constitution-aligned product resists trend-chasing that erodes trust

---

## Why intelligence will become commoditized

- Open weights, API competition, and distillation drive model costs down
- Capability gaps between top models shrink for most daily work
- "Smartest model" becomes temporary marketing, not durable advantage

**Implication:** Building a company on "we have the best model" is fragile. Building on **the continuity layer** is durable.

---

## Why continuity remains valuable

- Personal and professional AI work **accumulates** — decisions, drafts, context, relationships with one's assistant
- That accumulation is **painful to recreate** if lost — even when models are free
- Users will pay for **trust that it persists**, **freedom to export**, and **one assistant across change**

**The conversation never dies** is not a slogan — it is the economic thesis.
